# Cold email templates

Rules that keep these effective and safe:
- Send to businesses one at a time, personalised. No bulk lists or mass sending, and include a way to opt out.
- Keep it under 120 words. One clear ask.
- Only mention work that exists (your case studies). Never promise results you can't back up.

---

**Subject:** Quick idea for [company]'s [specific task]
