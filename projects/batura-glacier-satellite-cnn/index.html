Batura Glacier Satellite-Imagery CNN Analysis

A Python pipeline that builds half-month Sentinel-2 median composites of Batura Glacier (Hunza, Gilgit-Baltistan) in Google Earth Engine, exports them as GeoTIFFs and CNN-ready NumPy tile arrays, and checks every image for gaps and cloud. The CNN analysis of glacier change over 2020–2026 is in progress.
